Connecting Lives Through Blood Donation

Every Donation Saves Lives

Welcome to Blood Donors, a platform dedicated to connecting blood donors with people in need. Our system helps patients and their families find suitable blood donors based on blood group and location, making it easier to reach potential donors when every moment matters.

From finding available donors to submitting emergency blood requests, our goal is to make the blood donation process more accessible, convenient and efficient. We believe that every blood donation can make a difference, and together, we can help save lives by connecting those who are willing to donate with those who urgently need blood.

Blood Groups

Know. Match. Save Lives.

Explore our services and learn how we help save lives through blood donation.

  • A+ Blood Group

    Can donate red blood cells to A+ and AB+ individuals and is among the most common blood types worldwide.

  • A- Blood Group

    Helps A and AB recipients with either Rh type by providing compatible red blood cells for transfusions when needed.

  • B+ Blood Group

    Can donate red blood cells to B+ and AB+ individuals, helping patients with these compatible blood types during transfusions.

  • B- Blood Group

    Can help B and AB recipients with either Rh type by providing compatible red blood cells for patient transfusions.

  • AB+ Blood Group

    Known as the universal red blood cell recipient, it can receive red blood cells from all eight blood types.

  • AB- Blood Group

    A rare blood type that can donate red blood cells to both AB− and AB+ individuals, supporting compatible transfusions.

  • O+ Blood Group

    One of the most common blood types, it can donate red blood cells to all Rh-positive blood groups and patients.

  • O- Blood Group

    It is the universal red blood cell donor, especially important during emergencies when a patient's blood type remains unknown.
